# How a Robot Makes a Sandwich

## Assumptions

- The bread is on the counter.
- The peanut butter jar is closed.
- The jelly jar is closed.
- A butter knife is on the counter.
- The robot's hands are empty.
- Two slices of bread are available.

## Steps

1. Pick up the bread bag.
2. Open the bread bag.
3. Remove two slices of bread.
4. Place both slices flat on the counter.
5. Pick up the peanut butter jar.
6. Remove the lid from the peanut butter jar.
7. Pick up the butter knife.
8. Insert the knife into the peanut butter.
9. Scoop peanut butter onto the knife.
10. Spread the peanut butter across one slice of bread.
11. Put the knife down.
12. Close the peanut butter jar.
13. Pick up the jelly jar.
14. Remove the lid from the jelly jar.
15. Pick up the knife.
16. Scoop jelly onto the knife.
17. Spread the jelly across the second slice of bread.
18. Put the knife down.
19. Close the jelly jar.
20. Pick up one slice of bread.
21. Place it on top of the other slice with the peanut butter and jelly sides facing each other.

## Done When

The sandwich is finished when the two slices of bread are together with peanut butter and jelly between them.
